Privacy and approval notices

VELTHON Student Hub · Version 2026-10-29.1 · Notices in English.

These notices explain current app behaviour. Optional external providers require their own permission screens. Typed acknowledgments are account records, not certified proof of a physical signer’s identity. Before approving, read the scope and use your own account.

Privacy, local storage and activity records

Student Hub keeps study files, notes and recordings in an encrypted workspace on this device. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; export backups. The authentication service stores your account sign-ins, security events, consent decisions and basic activity types for up to 365 days. Activity logs do not include document text, recordings, search words, passwords, access tokens or full IP addresses. Consent receipts include your typed account name, policy text/version and timestamps. Optional external services have separate approval screens. You can review and export your own records or withdraw approval in Privacy & activity. Withdrawal stops future use of the relevant feature; it does not erase existing receipts, previously exported copies or provider records. Offline decisions are device-reported and become server-confirmed only after syncing. A typed acknowledgment records an account action; it is not verified identity or a certified digital signature.

Permission to record this lecture

I have permission to record this lecture and have checked the instructor, institution and class rules, including any required permission from other people being recorded. Student Hub cannot obtain that permission for me. Audio and transcription are processed and saved on this device unless I separately choose an external service or cloud export. This approval applies to the lecture reference displayed here. I can stop recording or withdraw this approval. Withdrawing does not delete existing recordings or copies.

Connect an external calendar

I choose to contact the selected Google or Microsoft calendar provider. Its sign-in helper receives connection information and may use cookies. I will select my own provider account and review its read-only calendar permission screen. Student Hub does not request my provider password or permission to read email messages. Calendar names, event titles, dates and locations can be displayed and cached in my local workspace. This app approval does not replace provider OAuth consent. Disconnecting stops app access and removes the app’s cached calendar entries; provider permissions can also be revoked with the provider.

Open an optional music service

I choose to open the selected music provider or load its embedded player. The provider receives connection information and may use cookies under its own policy. Playback, sign-in and subscription restrictions are controlled by that provider. Student Hub does not request my music password, payment details or music-library access. I can remove the player or disconnect in the app, and manage any provider sign-in with the provider.

Search or play external study videos

I choose to send my current search words or selected video request to the displayed study/video provider. That provider receives connection information and may use cookies. External video, search and account rules apply. Student Hub does not send my study documents, microphone recordings, calendar or email to the video provider. Search words are not stored in the Student Hub activity log or consent receipt. I can cancel, close playback or withdraw this app approval; this does not revoke a separate provider login.

Save a selected file to external storage

I choose to send the file I select to Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud or the device’s Files/share provider shown in the save flow. The selected file and connection information may be received by that provider. I must use my own account and review its permissions; Student Hub does not request my provider password. Files already sent remain with the destination until I remove them there. I can cancel before saving, choose a local destination instead, and withdraw app approval for future cloud transfers.

Earlier browser-only approval flags are not server-verified receipts. Current notices must be approved to create a new receipt. Offline workspaces cannot receive remote withdrawals until they reconnect.
